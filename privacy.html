Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Wrap up turns your own YouTube data export into a summary. This page explains exactly what happens to your data: in short, it stays on your device.

What you give us

You choose a file from Google Takeout (your watch history and, optionally, your subscriptions list) and drop it on the page. The page reads it with your browser. There is no sign-in and no connection to your Google or YouTube account.

What happens to it

  • Your file is processed entirely in your browser. It is not uploaded, and this site has no server, database or analytics. The page's security policy forbids network requests, so it can't send your data anywhere, and you can verify this in your browser's developer tools.
  • If you leave "Remember my wrap in this browser" ticked, a summary is saved in your browser's local storage on this device: counts, top channels, topics, activity patterns and your most-watched video titles. The raw file and your full history are never saved.
  • You can remove the saved summary at any time with "Forget my data" in the menu, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Estimates such as your rank in India are calculated from your own numbers and public figures. Your data is not compared with anyone else's.

Sharing

Share cards are images generated in your browser; they are only downloaded or shared when you choose to. "Copy share link" creates a link that contains your summary statistics (counts, top channels, topics and activity patterns) inside the part of the web address after the # symbol, which browsers don't send to servers. It does not contain video titles or your history. Anyone you give the link to can see those statistics, so share it only with people you trust, and note that links can't be recalled once sent.

Cookies and third parties

Wrap up sets no cookies and has no analytics. The page loads one font (Funnel Sans) from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers see a standard font request from your browser. Links to Takeout, YouTube and Google open on their own sites under their own policies: the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Wrap up is not directed at children under 13.
