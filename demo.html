Get your data in 5 minutes

  1. Open Google TakeoutGo to takeout.google.com and sign in.
  2. Pick only YouTubeClick Deselect all, then tick YouTube and YouTube Music.
  3. Choose what to includeClick All YouTube data included, press Deselect all, then tick history and subscriptions. Press OK.
  4. Switch History to JSONClick Multiple formats, change History from HTML to JSON, then OK. This step matters: we can't read the HTML version.
  5. Create the exportClick Next step, keep Export once and .zip, then Create export. Google emails you a download link, usually within minutes (sometimes up to a day).
  6. Drop the .zip hereDownload it and drop it in the box. No need to unzip.
Drop your Takeout .zip here or click to choose a file Works with the .zip as downloaded, or the unzipped watch-history.json (and subscriptions.csv).

Your file is read in your browser and never uploaded. This page isn't allowed to make network requests, which you can check in your browser's developer tools. Watch history must be switched on in your YouTube settings, otherwise the export is empty. Google sometimes rewords these buttons; the file we need is called watch-history.json.
